## 5. Findings confirmed

VER-01 confirms the following material findings:

1. The three lanes are economically and operationally distinct businesses and must remain separately labeled, contracted, accounted for, and represented publicly.
2. Lane A is publisher-funded and carries title-investment, channel, returns, royalty, and cash-flow risk that must be underwritten title by title.
3. KDP/Ingram/direct-sales economics cannot be treated as one blended margin assumption.
4. Authors Guild royalty figures are properly used as benchmarks rather than proposed Ekewaka terms.
5. Lane B can be a legitimate publishing model only if Ekewaka actually performs publisher functions and satisfies a substantially higher professional/ethical burden than fee-for-service work.
6. Lane B should remain research-only at this stage.
7. Lane C is economically plausible but service margins are highly sensitive to editing labor, revisions, creative scope, vendor costs, included copies, project management, refunds, chargebacks, and support burden.
8. Competitor sticker prices are not a defensible basis for Ekewaka package pricing without bottom-up internal costing.
9. FR-WP-06 should model lane/channel economics separately and must not be finalized before FR-WP-03 and FR-WP-05 are sufficiently mature.
10. No FIN-01 recommendation was found to have been converted into a reserved Human Owner approval.
