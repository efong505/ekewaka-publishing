## 5. Editing verification result

**Verified.**

Reedsy current 2026 editing guidance independently confirms the package's cited ranges:

- editorial assessment: 1.8–2.9 cents/word;
- developmental editing: 2.6–5.3 cents/word;
- copy editing: 2.0–3.2 cents/word;
- proofreading: 1.4–2.1 cents/word;
- approximately $1,920–$4,560 for one professional editorial engagement on a generic 80,000-word manuscript depending on service/scope.

EFA's 2026 survey independently confirms nonfiction copyediting at 3.0–4.0 cents/word, $45–$60/hour and 5–9 pages/hour. Its survey methodology and caveat that EFA does not set rates are preserved correctly.

The package correctly separates assessment, developmental editing, line/copyediting, proofreading and fact/citation support rather than using an undefined generic "editing" scope.

### Arithmetic recheck

Using the EFA 250-words/page convention:

- 80,000 words = 320 pages;
- copyedit at 5–9 pages/hour = 35.6–64 hours, reasonably rounded to 36–64;
- developmental edit at 4–8 pages/hour = 40–80 hours;
- formatting at 7–15 pages/hour = 21.3–45.7 hours, reasonably rounded to 21–46.

The derived labor-hour sensitivities are correct and properly classified as planning benchmarks, not staffing commitments.
