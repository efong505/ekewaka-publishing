- spelling/phonetic/spacing variants;
- materially similar live or pending marks in publishing, printed matter, downloadable publications/media, education, retail/author services and adjacent creative/media services;
- TSDR review for material hits sufficient to identify owner, status, goods/services and relevant priority/use information.

This remains preliminary screening; attorney-grade clearance and likelihood-of-confusion analysis remain open professional-review items even after the correction.
