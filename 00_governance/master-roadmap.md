# Ekewaka Publishing — Master Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Project Initialization
Status: **Complete — Proceed to Foundational Research**

Outputs: project charter, authoritative-source policy, agent architecture, roadmap, decision register, risk register, work-package register, Human Owner gates, and first research handoff.

## Phase 1 — Foundational Research
- FR-WP-01 Legal, Entity, Name & Brand Clearance Foundations
- FR-WP-02 Publishing Business Model & Industry Benchmark
- FR-WP-03 Printing, Distribution & Fulfillment Baseline
- FR-WP-04 Author Services Competitive Benchmark
- FR-WP-05 ISBN, Metadata & Rights Foundations
- FR-WP-06 Foundational Financial Model

## Phase 2 — Business & Brand Architecture
Legal-entity recommendation, brand/name recommendation, publishing mission, catalog scope, three-lane model, business-formation checklist, Human Owner decision gate.

## Phase 3 — Publishing Operating Model
Acquisitions, editorial, production, ISBN, metadata, rights, contracts, printing, distribution, royalties, author relations, quality control.

## Phase 4 — Services & Package Model
Packages, à-la-carte services, pricing, delivery processes, profitability, agreement framework, trade/services separation.

## Phase 5 — Marketing & Sales Architecture
Brand launch plan, publisher-site requirements, author recruitment architecture, title marketing system, direct sales, bookstore/library/institutional sales, measurement.

## Phase 6 — Software System Architecture
Requirements, data model, workflows, security, integrations, MVP boundaries, costs, independent review.

## Phase 7 — Business Formation / Operational Readiness
Execute only Human Owner-approved registrations, purchases, accounts, insurance/vendor relationships, ISBN acquisition, and professionally reviewed materials.

## Phase 8 — MVP Implementation
Website and operational system implementation, configured workflows and registries, end-to-end testing.

## Phase 9 — Founding Catalog Migration
Inventory Edward Fong titles and ISBN provenance; determine edition treatment and future Ekewaka migration title by title.

## Phase 10 — First Outside-Author Pilot
Bounded candidate solicitation, complete acquisition review, Human Owner approval of any offer, heightened documentation, independent retrospective.

## Phase 11 — Services Pilot
Only after packages, contracts, accounting, and production workflows are ready; bounded client count; measure labor, margin, satisfaction, and operational load.

## Phase 12 — Scale
Submission expansion only when capacity supports it; catalog growth; channel expansion; staffing/contractor network; possible hybrid lane; possible in-house printing feasibility study.

## Critical dependencies
- FR-WP-01 informs final entity/name/brand architecture.
- FR-WP-02, FR-WP-03, and FR-WP-05 materially feed FR-WP-06.
- FR-WP-06 must precede final acquisition economics, royalty structures, and service pricing.
- Phase 2 decisions precede operational formation actions.
- Phases 3 and 4 precede author acquisition or services launch.
- Phase 6 architecture must be independently reviewed before production implementation.
- Phase 7 gates precede external operational commitments.
- Phase 8 must be verified before pilots.
