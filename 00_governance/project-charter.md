# Ekewaka Publishing — Project Charter

## Project objective
Research, design, establish, implement, operate, market, and progressively scale Ekewaka Publishing as a selective independent publishing company capable of publishing Edward Fong titles, selectively acquiring outside authors, offering clearly separated author services, potentially evaluating a transparent hybrid/co-investment lane, and operating the business systems needed for rights, ISBNs, metadata, production, distribution, royalties, accounting, marketing, analytics, and auditability.

## Human Owner
Edward Fong is the Human Owner and final material decision authority.

## Working identity
- Project: Ekewaka Publishing
- Intended publishing identity: Ekewaka Publishing
- Preferred legal-entity concept to evaluate: Ekewaka Publishing LLC

These are working concepts, not final legal, trademark, tax, or entity determinations.

## Three-lane architecture
### Lane A — Ekewaka Select / Trade Publishing
Selective publisher-funded acquisition. Author does not pay a publication fee. Ekewaka funds approved production, controls publisher ISBNs for editions it publishes, obtains defined publishing rights by contract, and pays contractual royalties.

### Lane B — Ekewaka Partner / Hybrid or Co-Investment
Research only initially. No launch without explicit Human Owner approval and independent validation of professional, ethical, economic, and contractual defensibility.

### Lane C — Ekewaka Author Services
Paid professional services. Service clients remain their own publisher unless separately contracted. Payment for services must never imply acquisition into Ekewaka Select.

## Phase 0 prohibitions
No LLC filing, trademark filing, ISBN purchase, domain/service purchase, prospective-author contact, publishing agreement, publication of another author's work, paid-services launch, paid marketing, bulk print commitment, or public launch.
