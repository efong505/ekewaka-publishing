## Assignment
**FR-WP-01 — Legal, Entity, Name & Brand Clearance Foundations**

This is foundational research only.

## Required scope
### A. New Mexico business/entity research
Research current authoritative sources for LLC formation, Secretary of State requirements, entity-name rules/availability, registered-agent requirements, maintenance/reporting obligations, trade-name/DBA considerations, state trademark mechanisms, and relevant registrations.

Evaluate at minimum:
- Ekewaka Publishing
- Ekewaka Publishing LLC
- Ekewaka Press

Do not reserve or register any name.

### B. Albuquerque/local requirements
Determine applicable or potentially applicable municipal registration, business licensing, home-business/zoning or home-occupation requirements, and other local requirements. Distinguish confirmed requirements from fact-dependent issues.

### C. Federal foundations
Research EIN considerations, basic federal tax-classification issues relevant to plausible LLC structures, information-reporting considerations for royalties and contractor payments, and other foundational federal compliance matters. Do not apply for an EIN or make tax elections.

### D. New Mexico tax foundations
Research current authoritative information on gross receipts tax and material treatment questions involving physical books, direct retail, online/marketplace sales, publishing/creative services, royalties where relevant, deductions/exemptions, and marketplace-facilitator considerations. Separate confirmed rules, likely applicability, unresolved facts, and CPA-review items.

### E. Preliminary name/trademark clearance
Perform documented preliminary research for the three working names and materially similar/confusing publishing-related names across New Mexico records, USPTO, common-law/publishing use, book/imprint/catalog use, relevant adjacent industries, domains, and social/platform naming conflicts.

Do not claim legal clearance from absence of an exact match. Recommend whether attorney-level clearance is warranted and identify likely filing/class considerations only for later professional review.

### F. Publishing-specific legal foundation
Identify later framework/professional-review needs involving trade publishing, author services, possible hybrid agreements, copyright/publishing rights, subsidiary rights, termination/reversion, author warranties, permissions, royalties/audit rights, returns, contractors, privacy/ecommerce, AI use, generated artwork, manuscript confidentiality, voice cloning/audiobooks, and insurance.

Do not draft final contracts in FR-WP-01.

### G. Professional adviser / insurance needs
Identify where review may be warranted from a New Mexico business attorney, publishing/IP attorney, trademark attorney, CPA/tax adviser, bookkeeper, and insurance broker. Identify relevant insurance categories without purchasing coverage.
