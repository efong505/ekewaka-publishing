## Objective
Build a source-traceable foundational financial model for Ekewaka Publishing that can support later Human Owner decisions about sustainability, title underwriting, pricing architecture, royalty architecture, reserves, operating capacity, and lane economics without itself approving any of those decisions.

The model must preserve the project's three-lane separation:
- **Lane A — Ekewaka Select / Trade Publishing**
- **Lane B — Ekewaka Partner / Hybrid / Co-Investment** — research-only
- **Lane C — Ekewaka Author Services**

## Required financial architecture
At minimum model and clearly distinguish:

### Lane A / title economics
- list price by format;
- channel gross revenue;
- print/manufacturing cost;
- retailer/distributor/wholesale deductions;
- wholesale discounts;
- returnability and return reserves;
- shipping/freight/fulfillment where applicable;
- editorial and production investment;
- cover/interior/project-management costs;
- marketing allocation;
- ISBN/metadata/rights administration;
- permissions/counsel costs where applicable;
- author royalty basis and rate as scenario variables only;
- advances only as scenario variables if included;
- contribution margin;
- title break-even units/revenue;
- cash-at-risk;
- payback timing;
- downside and upside scenarios.

### Distribution/channel scenarios
Model separately where evidence supports:
- Amazon/KDP POD;
- KDP Expanded Distribution;
- Ingram trade/POD;
- Lulu Global Distribution;
- direct POD / Lulu Direct-like fulfillment;
- stocked direct inventory;
- events/churches/institutions/bulk sales;
- short-run digital;
- offset/large-run inventory.

Do not collapse materially different channels into a single generic distribution margin.

### Returns and inventory
- return-rate assumptions as clearly labeled sensitivities, not facts unless supported;
- wholesale-value chargeback exposure;
- applicable return shipping/handling;
- inventory carrying cost;
- shrink/damage/obsolescence;
- warehousing/3PL categories;
- freight;
- cash-conversion timing;
- reserve treatment.

### Lane B research-only economics
Model only as a research sensitivity framework. Do not approve or launch Lane B.
Where useful, model:
- publisher contribution;
- author contribution;
- professional production cost;
- distribution/sales obligations;
- royalty/revenue share sensitivity;
- break-even and author/publisher exposure;
- transparency requirements;
- downside/reputational risk.

Do not establish final hybrid fees, packages, terms, or promises.

### Lane C / author-services economics
Model foundational cost architecture only, including:
- internal labor;
- contractor/vendor cost;
- project management;
- word-count and complexity drivers;
- revision rounds;
- scope-change allowance;
- payment processing;
- refunds/chargebacks;
- customer support;
- taxes/GRT as unresolved or scenario inputs pending professional review;
- gross margin and minimum-price-floor mechanics.

FR-WP-04 remains a separate future work package and may later refine service-market benchmarks and package architecture. Do not treat FR-WP-06 as a substitute for FR-WP-04.
