# P3-WP-01 — Printing, Distribution, and Fulfillment Workflow Requirements

DIST-01 must design a decision workflow comparing appropriate channels and methods, including KDP, Ingram, direct/Lulu where relevant, short-run, offset, inventory, and 3PL options without selecting a vendor.

Required decision evidence:
- format/spec compatibility and channel reach;
- live print/unit/handling/freight/storage/3PL costs;
- discounts, returns exposure, reserves, and cash timing;
- order quantities and inventory risk;
- service levels, data/export portability, and account ownership;
- metadata/file dependencies and channel setup sequence;
- direct-sales fulfillment needs where applicable.

Every material vendor/platform/landed-cost/returns assumption requires live recheck before reliance. FIN-01 validates economics. Human Owner approval is required before material vendor contracts, bulk print commitments, or material funds. No purchase order, print order, account commitment, or vendor selection is authorized.
