# P3-WP-01 — ISBN, Metadata, and Rights Workflow Requirements

RIGHTS-01 must design:
- ISBN/format/edition decision records and no-reuse controls;
- a canonical title/format/edition metadata registry with controlled identifiers and change history;
- a rights registry covering grant, territory, language, format, exclusivity, term, subsidiary rights, retention, and restrictions;
- permissions/provenance records for text, images, quotations, generated or AI-assisted material, and third-party assets;
- reversion, termination, takedown, and wind-down controls;
- account-ownership, recovery, and access-boundary records;
- channel metadata synchronization and exception handling.

## Lane control
Publisher-controlled ISBNs/accounts apply only where Ekewaka is publisher of record under separately authorized arrangements. Lane C must preserve customer-controlled identities/accounts/ISBNs when the service architecture requires that posture. Service permissions are not publishing-rights grants.

No ISBN purchase, assignment, registration action, or live account configuration is authorized. Bowker/platform/ISBN rules must be live-rechecked before material reliance.
