# P3-WP-01 — Editorial, Production, and Quality Workflow Requirements

Phase 3 execution must define:
- developmental editing, copyediting, author review, proofreading, and final correction stages;
- role ownership, acceptance criteria, and version/status transitions for every stage;
- production specifications for print interiors, covers, and ebooks, including accessibility and complex-format considerations;
- canonical source-file ownership, naming, storage, backup/export, provenance, and recovery controls;
- proof generation, proof approval, correction logs, and bounded re-proof triggers;
- change-control rules distinguishing editorial correction, metadata correction, format/edition-impacting change, and post-release correction;
- QC checklists for content completeness, typography, links, images, permissions, accessibility, files, and channel compatibility;
- release-readiness evidence consumed by the final gate.

PROD-01 owns the architecture, coordinating with RIGHTS-01 for metadata/rights and DIST-01 for channel/file requirements. Vendor qualification may be modeled but no vendor may be selected or contracted.
