# P3-WP-01 — Acquisition and Author-Relations Workflow Requirements

Phase 3 execution must design, not operate:
1. controlled candidate/title intake with source and status;
2. evaluation criteria for mission/editorial fit, rights, production complexity, market evidence, capacity, economics, and risk;
3. acquisition memo with evidence, assumptions, unresolved items, and recommended disposition;
4. RIGHTS-01 rights/provenance review and FIN-01 economics gate;
5. LEGAL-01/CONTRACT-01 escalation where material;
6. PM-01 integration;
7. explicit Human Owner offer gate before any offer or acquisition;
8. author onboarding architecture only, including contacts, milestones, deliverables, rights records, communication cadence, and escalation;
9. lifecycle states for prospect, evaluating, hold, decline, approved-for-offer, contracted only after separate authority, active, release, post-release, reversion/termination/wind-down;
10. rejection/hold/decline controls and retention/privacy rules.

No general submissions launch, outreach campaign, offer, acquisition, negotiation, or onboarding may occur under preparation authority. Lane C customer intake must not be represented as acquisition and remains outside activation scope.
