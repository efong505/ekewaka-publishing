# P3-WP-01 — Royalty and Accounting Operations Workflow Requirements

ROY-01 with FIN-01 must design title/edition/channel ledger architecture, revenue-source mapping, royalty-basis data structure, return/reserve/accrual treatment, statement controls, reconciliation, approval checkpoints, audit history, record retention, correction/reissue procedures, cash-timing analysis, and strict separation of Lane A book revenue from Lane C service revenue.

Final contractual rates and reserve percentages remain outside this work package. CPA, tax, and accounting review must be routed at applicable material gates. This architecture does not create bank accounts, move funds, deploy production accounting systems, make tax elections, or approve final royalty, reserve, or pricing terms.
