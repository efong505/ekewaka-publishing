# P3-WP-01 — Release Readiness and Quality Gate Framework

Phase 3 execution must define a pre-publication gate requiring evidence that editorial and production stages are complete; canonical files and proofs are controlled; metadata and ISBN/edition decisions are coherent; rights, permissions, and provenance are complete; distribution and channel requirements are satisfied; current economics and returns assumptions are documented where material; required professional reviews are complete; privacy and security controls are satisfied for sensitive operational records; unresolved critical risks have disposition; and the Human Owner has explicitly approved publication where required.

Gate states: NOT READY, CORRECTION REQUIRED, READY FOR VERIFICATION, VERIFIED / VERIFIED WITH CONDITIONS, and HUMAN OWNER DECISION REQUIRED.

Post-release architecture must include issue intake, severity, correction/takedown escalation, metadata/file replacement governance, accounting-correction controls, and audit preservation. This framework does not authorize publication.
