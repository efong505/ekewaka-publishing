# P3-WP-01 — Scope and Boundaries

**Work package:** Publishing Operating Model  
**Lifecycle state:** PREPARATION COMPLETE — EXECUTION AUTHORIZATION PENDING  
**Preparation baseline:** `a20936185732624d94926b6a6a78a7d23caeb329`

## Objective
If separately authorized, P3-WP-01 will design the bounded operating model for acquisitions and author relations, editorial and production, ISBN/metadata/rights, contract-framework routing, printing/distribution/fulfillment, royalty/accounting operations, release readiness, and quality control. It will create workflows, controls, registers, templates, handoffs, readiness criteria, decision records, and verification evidence only.

## Lane architecture
- Lane A: core publisher-funded trade publishing.
- Lane B: dormant / research-only.
- Lane C: optional, demand-led, parallel author-services architecture subject to separate future authorization.

The operating model must keep separate: acquisitions vs. services; authors vs. customers; publishing rights vs. service permissions; publisher-controlled ISBN/accounts vs. Lane C customer-controlled identities/accounts; and book-sale revenue vs. service revenue.

## Founding-catalog boundary
Edward Fong titles are the founding catalog posture. P3-WP-01 must not perform title migration. Title-by-title provenance, ISBN, edition, and migration decisions remain governed for Phase 9 unless a separately authorized bounded prerequisite is approved.

## Explicit exclusions
No outside-author acquisition or offer; contract execution; final royalty, advance, pricing, refund, reserve, or margin terms; ISBN purchase or assignment; vendor selection/contracting; print orders; Lane B/Lane C launch; LLC formation; trademark filing; bank/account opening; tax elections; outside-author publication; production financial-system deployment; paid marketing; or material spending.

## Evidence controls
Current laws, platform terms, prices, fees, ISBN rules, processor/vendor capabilities, landed costs, market conditions, and tax/accounting assumptions require live recheck at material reliance points. Recommendations remain distinct from approvals.
