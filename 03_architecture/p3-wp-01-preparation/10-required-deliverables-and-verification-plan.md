# P3-WP-01 — Required Deliverables and Verification Plan

If execution is separately authorized, PM-01 must integrate at minimum:
1. operating-model scope and boundary specification;
2. role/RACI and specialist handoff map;
3. acquisition and author-relations workflow, criteria, and acquisition-memo template;
4. editorial/production workflow, file/proof/change-control standard, and QC checklist;
5. ISBN/edition decision template plus canonical metadata, rights, and permissions/provenance registry specifications;
6. contract-framework requirements and professional-review routing matrix;
7. printing/distribution/fulfillment decision framework and live-recheck record template;
8. title-level accounting/royalty operating model, statement/reconciliation and approval controls;
9. release-readiness gate/checklist and post-release correction process;
10. condition/risk/Human Owner gate traceability matrix;
11. evidence/source register with dates and volatility flags;
12. integrated PM-01 reconciliation package for independent review.

## Verification
VER-01 independently checks completeness, internal consistency, evidence traceability, lane separation, condition/risk carry-forward, Human Owner gates, legal/professional routing, financial-control coherence, privacy/security escalation, and absence of unauthorized execution.

VER-01 dispositions: Verified; Verified With Conditions; Correction Required; Not Ready. Specialists may not self-verify. A Verified or Verified With Conditions result returns to PM-01 for acceptance/reconciliation; it does not itself authorize operations or reserved Human Owner decisions.
