# P3-WP-01 — Contract Framework and Professional Review Requirements

LEGAL-01 / CONTRACT-01 must produce attorney-review-ready architecture, not final legal advice or executable agreements.

Required agreement-framework categories include, as applicable: Lane A publishing agreement; contributor/contractor and production/vendor agreements; permissions/releases; confidentiality/data-handling terms; amendment, termination/reversion, and wind-down instruments; and later Lane C service agreement architecture only if separately in scope, with strict separation from publishing agreements.

Framework requirements must address rights grant/retention, delivery/acceptance, editorial/production responsibilities, warranties/indemnity concepts for counsel review, royalties/accounting/audit concepts, permissions, AI/content provenance, term/termination/reversion, confidentiality/privacy, dispute/governing-law considerations, signature authority, and version control.

Qualified business/publishing-IP/trademark counsel review must be routed at material legal gates. CPA/tax/accounting and insurance professional review must be routed where economics, reporting, tax treatment, payment operations, or risk transfer are material.

No contract may be executed and no signature authority is granted by P3-WP-01 preparation or architecture.
