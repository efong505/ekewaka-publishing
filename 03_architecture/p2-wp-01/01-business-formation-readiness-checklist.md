# P2-WP-01 — Non-Executing Business Formation / Readiness Checklist

**Status:** Planning checklist only — no execution authority.

This checklist identifies the recommended dependency sequence after future Human Owner approvals. Checking an item in planning does not authorize the underlying filing, purchase, account, contract, or launch.

| Sequence | Readiness item | Required before execution | Human Owner gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confirm final legal-entity and brand recommendation | VER-01 review of P2-WP-01; attorney/CPA conditions mapped | Final name/entity adoption |
| 2 | Fresh legal/name/trademark checks | Same-day NM/current-law checks; comprehensive trademark/common-law clearance | Name adoption / filing |
| 3 | Approve entity filing posture | NM attorney review; registered-agent/privacy/local-requirement review | LLC/entity filing |
| 4 | Determine tax/accounting posture | CPA review of tax classification, GRT, channel treatment, royalties, services, contractors | Tax election / registrations |
| 5 | File entity and obtain identifiers only after approval | Current filing requirements and approved sequence | Filing / EIN / tax registrations |
| 6 | Establish banking/bookkeeping controls | Entity/tax posture accepted; chart-of-accounts and separation requirements defined | Bank/financial account creation |
| 7 | Complete insurance review | Broker review for liability, media/E&O, professional, cyber/privacy as applicable | Insurance purchase |
| 8 | Decide ISBN block strategy | Live Bowker pricing/options; publisher identity confirmed | ISBN block purchase |
| 9 | Establish title/ISBN/metadata/rights registries | Data model and controls reviewed; no production deployment yet | Later system/deployment gate |
| 10 | Build attorney-review-ready contract frameworks | Lane-specific business architecture accepted | Contract execution remains separate |
| 11 | Validate distribution/procurement evidence | Live KDP/Ingram/Lulu costs; comparable vendor quotes; returns/3PL evidence | Vendor/print commitments |
| 12 | Finalize title-level financial controls | Resolve applicable VER06-C1/C2; current economics; CPA/accounting inputs | Prices/royalties/acquisition economics |
| 13 | Develop Lane C commercial architecture if later authorized | SERV/FIN/legal/tax/market-validation gates | Package pricing / public launch |
| 14 | Develop website/commerce/operational systems | Security/privacy/financial controls and independent review | Public launch / production deployment |
| 15 | Founding-catalog migration | Title-by-title ISBN provenance and edition decision | Publication/republication as applicable |
| 16 | Outside-author pilot | Phase 3+ operating model, contracts, economics, rights, production readiness | Acquisition / agreement / publication |

## Rules

- Lane B remains research-only unless separately approved.
- Lane C remains unlaunched until a later governed package explicitly reaches its Human Owner launch gate.
- No outside-author outreach/acquisition is authorized by this checklist.
- Current laws, fees, platform terms, tax rules, vendor capabilities, ISBN rules, and distribution terms must be refreshed at the material-decision point.
- Professional review conditions are mandatory at their stated gates and are not waived by Phase 2 architecture acceptance.
