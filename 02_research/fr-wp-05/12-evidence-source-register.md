## Evidence discipline
- Primary/authoritative sources were preferred for material ISBN, platform and copyright propositions.
- Platform policies are volatile and must be live-rechecked before implementation.
- Legal sources support identification of legal categories and statutory boundaries only; RIGHTS-01 does not convert them into individualized legal advice or final contract conclusions.
- Secondary commentary was not used as controlling authority where primary sources were available.
