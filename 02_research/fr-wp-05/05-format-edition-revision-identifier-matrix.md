# FR-WP-05 — Format, Edition & Revision Identifier Matrix

| Change / product | ISBN treatment baseline | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Paperback vs hardcover | Separate ISBNs | Distinct formats/bindings are separately identified products. |
| Print vs ebook | Usually separate ISBNs where ebook ISBN is used | KDP does not require an ISBN for ebooks; other channels may. |
| Audiobook | ISBN-eligible; treat as separate product | Audio rights are legally distinct from ISBN assignment and must be separately confirmed. |
| Distinct ebook formats/usage constraints | May require separate ISBNs | International ISBN guidance treats separately traded digital formats/usage constraints as distinct products. |
| Exact reprint, no substantive change | Retain ISBN | Minor typo correction generally does not itself create a new edition. |
| New/revised edition with substantial content changes | New ISBN | New edition is a different product. |
| Change of title/subtitle materially identifying product | New ISBN | ISBN agencies treat title change as requiring new identifier. |
| Change of publisher | New publisher assigns its own ISBN | ISBN identifies publisher as well as publication. |
| Price-only change | Same ISBN | Price changes alone do not require new ISBN. |
| Marketing-only change | Same ISBN absent qualifying product change | Do not issue new ISBN merely for promotion. |
| Different binding/spec that creates distinct trade product | New ISBN | Validate against current ISBN agency and channel rules. |

## Ekewaka production rule recommendation
Before any ISBN assignment, require an identifier decision record containing: title, edition, format, binding, publisher/imprint, planned channels, prior ISBN (if any), reason for new/same identifier treatment, source authority, and Human Owner approval where assignment/purchase is involved.

## Founding catalog migration dependency
Phase 9 should inventory every Edward Fong title's existing ISBN provenance and product state before any future Ekewaka migration. A publisher change can require a new publisher-owned ISBN for a new publication event; this must not be treated as a metadata-only swap.

## Boundary
This matrix is operational research, not legal advice and not an ISBN assignment decision for any specific book.
