# FR-WP-05 — Recommendations Separated From Approvals

## RIGHTS-01 recommendations
1. Prefer publisher-controlled ISBNs for future Lane A Ekewaka-published editions.
2. Maintain a central ISBN/edition ledger before any outside-author publication.
3. Maintain a canonical metadata registry plus channel-observed state and correction history.
4. Maintain a rights registry linked to title, contract, edition and revenue/accounting records.
5. Keep Lane A, Lane B and Lane C publisher-of-record, ISBN ownership, rights and accounting boundaries explicit.
6. Require an identifier decision record before every new ISBN assignment or decision to retain an existing ISBN.
7. Require publishing/IP counsel review of final rights-grant and contract frameworks.
8. Recheck live platform identifier/metadata rules immediately before implementation.
9. Treat platform-owned free ISBNs as an explicit strategic exception, not the default for Lane A.
10. Have FR-WP-06 model identifier, metadata administration, professional review and rights/edition lifecycle costs as configurable inputs.

## Not approved by this package
The following remain unapproved:
- purchase of any ISBN or ISBN block;
- assignment of any ISBN to any title;
- final Ekewaka imprint;
- final legal entity or publisher-of-record architecture;
- final KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu or other account architecture;
- final royalty rates or bases;
- advances or recoupment structures;
- final publishing, hybrid or services agreements;
- acquisition of any outside-author rights;
- Lane B or Lane C launch;
- publication of another author's work;
- trademark filings, domains, entity filing, payment/royalty systems or paid marketing.
