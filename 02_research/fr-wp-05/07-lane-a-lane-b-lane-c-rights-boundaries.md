# FR-WP-05 — Lane A / Lane B / Lane C Rights Boundaries

## Lane A — Ekewaka Select / Trade Publishing
Research baseline:
- Ekewaka acts as publisher, not merely service provider, for accepted editions.
- Publisher-controlled ISBNs are the preferred architecture for Ekewaka-published editions, subject to Human Owner approval.
- Author copyright may remain with the author while specifically defined publishing rights are licensed to Ekewaka.
- Contract must define rights granted/retained, exclusivity, formats, languages, territory, term, reversion/termination, royalty/accounting basis, permissions, audit, sublicensing and subsidiary-rights treatment.
- No acquisition offer or rights grant is authorized by this package.

## Lane B — Partner / Hybrid / Co-Investment
Status remains **research-only**.
If ever approved, the model must separately disclose:
- author financial contribution;
- publisher financial/editorial contribution;
- publisher-of-record and ISBN ownership;
- copyright and licensed rights;
- royalty/revenue share basis;
- recoupment, if any;
- term/reversion;
- service components versus publishing obligations;
- marketing/distribution limitations.

No Lane B launch, contract structure or economics are approved.

## Lane C — Author Services
Default research boundary:
- customer remains their own publisher unless a different publishing relationship is separately and explicitly approved;
- customer should normally control their ISBN/publishing identity where an ISBN is used;
- Ekewaka acts as a service provider, not publisher of record, unless a different relationship is clearly contracted and approved;
- service licenses should be narrow, nonexclusive where feasible, purpose-limited and time-bounded to performance of the service;
- paying for services must not imply trade acquisition, sales performance, bookstore placement or other publisher commitments.

## Cross-lane controls
- separate agreements/templates;
- separate onboarding disclosures;
- explicit publisher-of-record field;
- explicit ISBN owner field;
- explicit copyright owner field;
- explicit rights-grant record;
- separate accounting treatment;
- no silent migration between lanes;
- Human Owner approval for any outside-author publication or Lane B/Lane C launch.

## Risk linkage
These controls directly mitigate `RISK-002`, `RISK-004`, `RISK-006`, `RISK-009`, `RISK-011` and `RISK-013`.
