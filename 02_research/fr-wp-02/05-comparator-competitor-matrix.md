## Comparator interpretation

### Trade/small press

There is limited public, current, comparable contract economics from named small presses. FIN-01 therefore uses the Authors Guild model contract as a defensible industry benchmark and current platform economics for channel mechanics rather than pretending confidential publisher P&Ls are public.

### Hybrid

She Writes Press and Greenleaf are useful because they publicly identify as hybrid, describe selectivity/publisher responsibilities, and provide current author-control or fee information. They should not be treated as proof that their economics fit Ekewaka.

### Author services

Destiny House is directly relevant to the master-prompt requirement and shows a low-cost Christian self-publishing package architecture. BookBaby and Reedsy demonstrate that professional editorial/design labor can be far more expensive than low package sticker prices, reinforcing the need for internal costing.
