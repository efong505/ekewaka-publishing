# FR-WP-02 — Unresolved Questions / Gaps

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-08

## Open items that do not block foundational FR-WP-02 completion

1. **Exact IngramSpark discount/returns architecture.** FR-WP-03 must establish the current operational choices and their consequences before FR-WP-06 finalizes trade-channel assumptions.
2. **Current print-cost matrix by format and quantity.** FR-WP-03 owns POD, short-run, local and offset economics.
3. **ISBN/publisher-of-record rules by lane.** FR-WP-05 must validate the exact ISBN and metadata architecture; FR-WP-02 only establishes the preliminary business-model posture.
4. **Final royalty rates and bases.** Authors Guild figures are benchmarks, not Ekewaka terms. FR-WP-06 must test affordability and publishing counsel must review definitions.
5. **Advance policy.** $0 advance / first-sale royalty is an economically plausible startup concept but not an approved blanket policy.
6. **Lane B author-economics threshold.** “Higher than standard” requires a measurable Ekewaka benchmark if Lane B survives later design.
7. **Lane B publisher contribution.** No minimum publisher investment or at-risk value has been established.
8. **Lane B market validation.** Ekewaka has not demonstrated hybrid sales/distribution performance and therefore cannot presently satisfy the “respectable sales” dimension through its own track record.
9. **Lane C internal labor rates.** No validated loaded hourly rates or contractor panel costs yet exist.
10. **Lane C package scope.** SERV-01 owns later deliverables, limits and package architecture.
11. **Direct-commerce stack costs.** Payment processing, ecommerce platform, fraud/chargeback and fulfillment architecture require later DIST/WEB/SYS research.
12. **Tax/GRT treatment.** CPA review is required for direct sales, service revenue, author contributions, royalties, marketplace transactions and multistate activity.
13. **Contract accounting definitions.** Returns reserve, net receipts, royalty statement timing, audit rights and refunds require publishing-counsel/CPA review.
14. **Competitor price volatility.** Destiny House displayed inconsistent main-page versus individual-product prices; all competitor pricing must be live-rechecked immediately before package positioning decisions.
15. **Small-press named-contract comparators.** Public current contracts from named small presses are limited; this package appropriately uses Authors Guild model terms as a benchmark rather than inventing confidential competitor economics.
