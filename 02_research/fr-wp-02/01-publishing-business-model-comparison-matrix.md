# FR-WP-02 — Publishing Business-Model Comparison Matrix

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-08

| Dimension | Traditional / Trade Publisher | Independent / Small Press | Reputable Hybrid / Co-Investment | Author-Services / Assisted Self-Publishing | Direct Self-Publishing / DTC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who pays publishing cost | Publisher | Publisher | Author subsidy plus publisher responsibility/investment | Author/customer | Author/publisher |
| Manuscript selection | Selective | Selective | Must be meaningfully vetted under IBPA criteria | Usually customer eligibility/scope fit rather than acquisition | Author decides |
| Primary commercial risk | Publisher | Publisher | Shared; author materially exposed | Author bears title sales risk; service provider bears delivery/scope risk | Author bears title risk |
| Copyright | Typically author-owned; publisher receives licensed rights | Same general posture | Contract-specific; strong comparators emphasize author ownership | Author retains rights | Author retains rights |
| ISBN | Publisher/imprint ISBN | Publisher/imprint ISBN | IBPA expects hybrid publisher's imprint/ISBN | Prefer author/customer ISBN or clearly disclosed service-provider ISBN treatment | Author's ISBN or platform-provided ISBN |
| Distribution accounts | Publisher controls | Publisher controls | Publisher controls/operates distribution | Prefer author-controlled accounts; provider may set up on author's behalf | Author controls |
| Primary provider revenue | Book sales and rights | Book sales and rights | Author contribution + book/rights revenue | Fees for services; possible vendor markup | Book sales |
| Author payment | Advance if any + royalties | Often no/low advance + royalties | Higher-than-standard royalty/proceeds expected | Author receives title revenues after platforms/channel costs | Author receives platform payout |
| Wholesale/returns risk | Publisher | Publisher | Publisher/shared by contract | Normally author if distribution is theirs | Author |

| Ekewaka analogue | Lane A | Lane A | Lane B | Lane C | Can be channel architecture supporting Lane A/C |

## Critical classification rule

A fee paid by an author does not by itself define whether a relationship is hybrid or services. The decisive questions are who is the publisher of record, whose ISBN and imprint are used, who controls the distribution account, who accepts publishing responsibility, what rights are licensed, and whether the provider participates in title sales economics.

For Ekewaka, that means:

- **Lane A:** no author publication fee; Ekewaka is publisher.
- **Lane B:** if ever approved, author contribution must be explicit and Ekewaka still acts as a true publisher, including ISBN/imprint, production, distribution and sales responsibility.
- **Lane C:** author buys professional deliverables but remains publisher; Ekewaka should not imply acquisition, use publisher control unnecessarily, or create sales guarantees.
