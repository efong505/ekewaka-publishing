## Source-quality conclusions

### Primary/current evidence is sufficient for foundational channel mechanics

KDP, IngramSpark and Lulu provide direct current evidence for the core payout/discount/returns/direct-fulfillment structures needed to seed FR-WP-06 sensitivities. Exact title print costs and Ingram operational choices remain downstream FR-WP-03 tasks.

### Professional associations provide defensible business-model/contract benchmarks

IBPA and the Authors Guild provide current, transparent standards and model-contract benchmarks. Their material is used as an industry benchmark and risk-control reference, not as final Ekewaka contract language.

### Provider comparators are examples, not confidential economic evidence

She Writes Press, Greenleaf, Destiny House and BookBaby are used only for what their current public pages disclose. FR-WP-02 does not infer provider profit, author ROI, private royalty deals, cost of labor or unpublished acceptance economics.

### Secondary market data is explicitly bounded

Reedsy is used for professional service-cost context. Society of Authors/Writers' Guild evidence is used for reputational/consumer-practice risk context. Neither is treated as a binding U.S. legal or universal market rule.

## Freshness requirement

All prices, platform rates, discounts, fees, payment timing and provider package claims in this register are retrieval-date evidence only. Material downstream decisions must reverify current terms on the decision date.
