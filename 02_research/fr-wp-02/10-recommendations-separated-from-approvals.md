# FR-WP-02 — Recommendations Separated From Approvals

**Agent:** FIN-01  
**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-08  
**Governance rule:** Nothing in this document constitutes approval of a Human Owner-gated action.

## A. FIN-01 recommendations

### R-01 — Use Lane A as the primary model for later publishing architecture

Proceed with **Ekewaka Select / Trade Publishing** as the principal model to develop in later phases, subject to title-level acquisition economics, rights/contract review, distribution validation, and Human Owner approval before any outside-author offer.

Rationale: the publisher-funded model is the cleanest fit for a selective press and creates the least ambiguity regarding who is taking publication risk.

### R-02 — Keep Lane B research-only

Do not market, sell, pilot, or operationalize **Ekewaka Partner / Hybrid / Co-Investment** at this stage.

Any future Lane B design should be conditioned on:

- FR-WP-03 distribution/returns evidence;
- FR-WP-05 ISBN/rights evidence;
- FR-WP-06 validated economics;
- a measurable higher-than-trade author economic proposition;
- a real publisher contribution/responsibility model;
- IBPA-level professional-standard mapping;
- attorney/CPA review;
- explicit Human Owner approval.

### R-03 — Advance Lane C to later SERV-01 design with strict separation

Proceed later with **Ekewaka Author Services** package architecture only as a separately labeled fee-for-service business relationship.

Recommended default controls:

- author/customer remains publisher;
- customer-owned ISBN and publishing accounts wherever practical;
- customer retains title revenue directly where practical;
- defined deliverables, word/page limits and revision limits;
- vendor allowances and change-order rules;
- explicit refund/cancellation treatment;
- no implied acquisition into Lane A;
- no sales, bestseller, review or bookstore-placement guarantees.

### R-04 — Build FR-WP-06 by lane and channel, not as one blended margin model

FR-WP-06 should separately model:

- Lane A Amazon/KDP;
- Lane A Ingram/trade;
- Lane A direct;
- Lane A bulk/institutional;
- Lane A author copies;
- Lane B author contribution and title economics only as a research scenario if PM retains it;
- Lane C service labor/vendor economics;
- company-level fixed overhead/cash runway.

### R-05 — Use contribution margin and cash timing as acquisition controls

Do not assess title viability from list price or gross sales alone. Each acquisition model should account for channel payout, print cost, returns, author royalty, title investment, marketing, overhead allocation and payment timing.

### R-06 — Treat direct sales as a first-class economic channel

Direct commerce can improve unit contribution but creates fulfillment, payment, refund, support and tax obligations. It should therefore receive dedicated DIST/MKT/WEB/SYS architecture rather than being treated as an informal side channel.

### R-07 — Derive author-service price floors from internal cost before market positioning

SERV-01 should first define scope. FIN-01/FR-WP-06 should then calculate fully loaded cost and price floors. Competitor sticker prices should be used for positioning only after internal economics are known.

### R-08 — Preserve professionally reviewed contract/accounting definitions

Publishing counsel and CPA/accounting review should be required before final adoption of:

- royalty bases and net-receipts definitions;
- returns reserves;
- subsidiary-right allocations;
- author-copy treatment;
- hybrid contributions/refunds if ever used;
- service deposits/refunds;
- tax/GRT treatment;
- royalty statements and payment timing.

### R-09 — Recheck volatile market/platform rates immediately before downstream decisions

KDP, IngramSpark, Lulu, competitor package pricing, print rates, payment terms and other current economic inputs must be live-rechecked before FR-WP-06 final acceptance and again before any operational launch or pricing publication.
