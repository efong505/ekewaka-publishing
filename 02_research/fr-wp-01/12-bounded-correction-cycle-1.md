## CR-03 — New Mexico domestic LLC recurring SOS reporting

### Primary authority reviewed
- New Mexico SOS statute index: https://www.sos.nm.gov/business-services/statutes-governing-business-in-nm/
- Current LLC Act text/amendments: https://www.nmlegis.gov/sessions/03%20Regular/FinalVersions/SB0379.html
- SOS Business Services/resources pages.
- Historical legislative proposals for LLC biennial/triennial reporting were reviewed only to distinguish proposed legislation from current law.

### Result
The New Mexico SOS identifies the current Limited Liability Company Act as **NMSA 1978 §§53-19-1 through 53-19-74**. The current LLC Act reviewed contains no annual, biennial, triennial or similar recurring Secretary-of-State report provision for a domestic LLC. The SOS separately identifies the **Corporate Reports** statutes as Chapter 53, Article 5.

Historical proposals to add LLC periodic reports do not alter the current Act and are not treated as law.

**Bounded conclusion:** under current primary authority reviewed on 2026-09-07, a domestic New Mexico LLC does **not** have a recurring annual/biennial/triennial/periodic Secretary-of-State report requirement under the current LLC Act. This does not eliminate registered-office/registered-agent obligations or event-driven filings, and the law must be rechecked immediately before formation.

**CR-03 state: RESOLVED for FR-WP-01 foundational scope.**
