## Required facts before local compliance can be finalized

- Exact operating address.
- Whether the address is inside Albuquerque municipal boundaries.
- Residential versus commercial use.
- Number and frequency of customer/author/vendor visits.
- Inventory/storage quantities and delivery frequency.
- Any employees or contractors routinely working onsite.
- Signage.
- Events, signings, retail pickup, or public sales from the location.

## Recommendation

Before launch, submit the exact operating model/address for a zoning/license check with the City and have counsel review any unusual home-occupation constraints. No City application is authorized in FR-WP-01.
