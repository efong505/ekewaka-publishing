## Preliminary findings

Public-web/USPTO-domain searching did not surface an obvious federal registration or application for the exact strings **EKEWAKA PUBLISHING** or **EKEWAKA PRESS** in this research pass. That finding is limited: search-engine indexing is not a substitute for the USPTO Trademark Search system, TSDR records, design-code review, owner-name searching, or a professional likelihood-of-confusion analysis.

The dominant term **EKEWAKA** is not unique in commerce/culture. It appears as a Hawaiian personal name and is used by third parties in non-publishing and media contexts. Therefore the absence of an exact federal hit would not itself establish registrability or freedom to use.
