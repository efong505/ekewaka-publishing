# FR-WP-01 — Preliminary Tax / Compliance Matrix

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-07  
**Not tax advice. CPA review required before implementation.**

| Revenue / compliance area | Current foundation | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| NM business registration | NM TRD states anyone engaging in business in New Mexico must register and receive a New Mexico Business Tax Identification Number/accounts as applicable. | Confirmed |
| NM gross receipts tax | GRT applies broadly to receipts from selling property in NM and performing services in NM, subject to exemptions/deductions/sourcing rules. | Confirmed baseline |
| Direct retail book sales in NM | Physical books are property sales and are within the general GRT base unless a specific exemption/deduction applies. | Likely applicable; CPA confirm sourcing/rate |
| Direct ecommerce sales | Seller must map destination/sourcing, economic nexus, and other-state sales-tax obligations as volume expands. | Fact-dependent; multistate review later |
| Marketplace sales | NM marketplace rules recognize provider/seller treatment and potential seller deductions for marketplace-facilitated receipts. | Confirmed framework; platform-by-platform facts required |
| Amazon/KDP | Determine whether each transaction is a marketplace-facilitated retail sale, royalty/license payment, print-service transaction, or other settlement based on actual contracts/statements. | Unresolved; CPA/contract review |
| Ingram/wholesale | Determine seller-of-record, destination sourcing, resale/deduction documentation, returns and settlement treatment. | Unresolved; CPA review |
| Author-services revenue | Editing/design/formatting/consulting and related services fall within NM's broad service-receipts framework unless a specific exemption/deduction applies. | Likely taxable GRT; CPA confirm |
| Publishing royalties received | Character depends on contractual structure and whether payment is for licensing intangible rights versus sale/service activity. | Fact-dependent; CPA review |
| Royalties paid to authors | IRS Form 1099-MISC instructions identify publisher-paid copyright royalties as royalty reporting; current threshold rules must be checked annually. | Confirmed federal reporting category; annual threshold reverify |
| Contractor payments | IRS requires Form W-9 collection and Form 1099-NEC reporting when current requirements/thresholds are met. 2026 thresholds changed for certain payments, so use current-year instructions. | Confirmed framework |
| Backup withholding | Can apply to reportable payments including royalties and nonemployee compensation in specified circumstances. | Confirmed federal framework |
| Single-member LLC default | Generally disregarded for federal income tax unless corporate election. | Confirmed |
| Multi-member LLC default | Generally partnership for federal income tax unless corporate election. | Confirmed |
| S-corporation election | Potential option if eligible; shareholder-employees must receive reasonable compensation before non-wage distributions. | Optional; CPA analysis required |
| EIN | IRS advises forming the legal entity before applying. EIN is required in several situations and may be obtained for banking/state purposes even when not otherwise federally required. | Confirmed framework; exact need fact-dependent |
| BOI / CTA | FinCEN final rule effective August 2026 exempts U.S.-created companies from BOI reporting. | Confirmed current rule |
