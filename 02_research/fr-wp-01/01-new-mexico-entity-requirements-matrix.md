# FR-WP-01 — New Mexico Entity Requirements Matrix

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-07  
**Scope:** Foundational research; no filing performed.

| Topic | Current finding | Status | Source / note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formation channel | New Mexico SOS states business filings have moved to its online Enterprise filing portal. | Confirmed | https://www.sos.nm.gov/business-services/ |
| Governing law | SOS identifies the Limited Liability Companies Act as NMSA 1978 §§ 53-19-1 through 53-19-74. | Confirmed | https://www.sos.nm.gov/business-services/statutes-governing-business-in-nm/ |
| Articles of Organization | Statutory text requires an LLC name satisfying §53-19-3, initial registered office and agent, principal place of business if different, duration if not perpetual, manager-management statement where applicable, single-member statement where applicable, and optional provisions. | Confirmed | NM Legislature Final Version SB0379, §§ amending 53-19-8/9: https://www.nmlegis.gov/sessions/03%20Regular/FinalVersions/SB0379.html |
| Filing fee | Current SOS-filed domestic LLC articles evidence states non-refundable application fee of $50. | Confirmed current evidence | https://enterprise.sos.nm.gov/api/report/GetImageByNum/140048022070249061191125241036130034251214031174 |
| Registered office | LLC must maintain a registered office in New Mexico. | Confirmed | NM LLC Act text cited above. |
| Registered agent | Agent may be a New Mexico resident individual, qualifying domestic entity, or qualifying foreign entity with required NM place of business. | Confirmed | NM LLC Act text cited above. |
| Agent acceptance | Filing provisions require a statement of the appointed registered agent accepting appointment. | Confirmed | NM LLC Act text cited above. |
| Name availability | SOS Enterprise provides search for registered business, fictitious name, or trademark. Absence of an indexed web result is not a legal availability determination. | Confirmed process / unresolved result | https://www.sos.nm.gov/ ; https://enterprise.sos.nm.gov/ |
| Name reservation | NM statutory text provides a 120-day reservation mechanism when a name is available. | Confirmed mechanism | NM Legislature Final Version SB0379 cited above. **No reservation authorized or made.** |
| Fictitious name / DBA | SOS resources list an Application for Fictitious Name, and Enterprise can search fictitious names. Need counsel/SOS confirmation on whether a separate filing is needed for the final operating/imprint structure. | Confirmed mechanism; applicability fact-dependent | https://www.sos.nm.gov/business-services/resources/ |
| State trademark/service mark | NM SOS accepts state trademark/service-mark registrations; application requires use specimens and fee. Current page states minimum $50 including one class, +$25 each additional class. | Confirmed | https://www.sos.nm.gov/commercial-services/trademarks/ |
| LLC annual/biennial report | Current reviewed SOS materials clearly show annual/biennial reporting for corporations, but this research did not locate a current authoritative SOS statement imposing an analogous recurring report on domestic LLCs. | Unresolved / do not infer | Confirm directly with SOS or NM business counsel before formation. |
| Operating agreement | Not researched to final legal sufficiency in FR-WP-01; recommended even for a single-member publisher to define governance, succession, authority, IP/assets, and separateness. | Recommendation / attorney review | Not a filing action. |
| Entity type | New Mexico LLC remains a plausible fit for liability separation and operational simplicity, but sole proprietorship, LLC default taxation, and possible later S-corp tax election have different tax/admin consequences. | Recommendation pending CPA/attorney review | IRS LLC sources in evidence register. |
