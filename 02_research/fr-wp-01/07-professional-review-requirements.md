# FR-WP-01 — Professional Review Requirements

**Research date:** 2026-09-07

## New Mexico business attorney

Review before formation:
- LLC versus alternative state-law structure.
- Single-member governance, operating agreement, succession/incapacity, authority and asset separation.
- Registered-agent and home-address/privacy considerations.
- Final entity/name availability and filing sequence.
- Albuquerque home-business facts and any zoning/license edge cases.
- State fictitious-name/trade-name needs for any imprint structure.

## Publishing / IP attorney

Review before outside-author contracting or rights acquisition:
- Trade publishing agreement.
- Author-services agreement.
- Hybrid/co-investment agreement only if later approved.
- Precise copyright grant: formats, languages, territories, term, exclusivity, subsidiary rights, sublicensing and reserved rights.
- Termination, rights reversion, out-of-print definitions and post-termination inventory.
- Royalties, defined net receipts, reserves/returns, statements and audit rights.
- Author representations/warranties, indemnity, permissions, defamation/privacy/publicity and fact-risk allocation.
- Contractor/editor/designer IP ownership and work-made-for-hire/assignment language.
- AI disclosure/use, generated text/art, manuscript training restrictions, human review, voice cloning/audiobook rights and synthetic performance.
- Confidentiality and manuscript security where justified.
- Website terms/privacy/ecommerce and consumer-facing refund/returns rules.

## Trademark attorney

Review before final brand adoption, domain strategy tied to reliance on the mark, or filing:
- Comprehensive federal/state/common-law clearance for EKEWAKA and composite marks.
- Likelihood-of-confusion analysis.
- Registrability/disclaimer issues.
- Filing basis, owner identity, classes and identifications.
- Word mark versus logo sequencing.
- Monitoring/enforcement strategy appropriate to a small press.

## CPA / New Mexico tax adviser

Review before first taxable operation:
- Default LLC tax treatment versus S-corp election timing/economics.
- NM GRT registration, location codes, rates, sourcing and deductions.
- Direct book sales, events, bulk orders, ecommerce and digital products.
- Amazon/KDP, Ingram, Lulu and other marketplace/wholesale settlement treatment.
- Author-service revenue.
- Royalties received and paid.
- 1099-MISC/1099-NEC, W-9 and backup-withholding controls.
- Multistate sales-tax/economic-nexus monitoring.
- Owner draws/payroll, estimated taxes and reasonable compensation if S-corp elected.

## Bookkeeper / royalty accountant

Establish later operational controls for:
- title-level chart of accounts;
- royalties payable/accruals;
- returns/reserves;
- publisher versus service revenue separation;
- marketplace settlement reconciliation;
- author statements;
- source-document retention and audit trail.

## Insurance broker

Evaluate, without purchasing in FR-WP-01:
- Commercial general liability.
- Media liability / publishers errors & omissions.
- Professional liability for author-service work.
- Cyber/privacy coverage when systems store author/customer/contract/payment data.
- Property/inland marine if meaningful inventory/equipment is held.
- Workers' compensation/employment coverage if staffing triggers requirements.
- Event coverage for signings/fairs where venues require it.

## Professional-review gate

These reviews are recommendations and future gates, not authorization to retain or purchase professional services. Material engagement cost requires Human Owner approval under project governance.
