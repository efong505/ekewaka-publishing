# FR-WP-06 — Lane B Research-Only Sensitivity Model

**Status:** research-only. No Lane B launch, package, fee, offer, term, or promise is authorized.

## Variables
Publisher contribution `P`, author contribution `A`, professional production cost `C`, distribution/marketing obligation `D`, returns/refund reserve `R`, publisher revenue share `s_p`, author revenue share `s_a`, and realized sales `Q`.

Publisher cash exposure before sales: `max(0, P + publisher-paid C/D/R − author funds contractually and legally available for those purposes)`.

Publisher break-even: `publisher at-risk cash / publisher contribution per realized unit`, only when contribution is positive.

## Required sensitivities
- author contribution: low/base/high;
- publisher at-risk contribution: low/base/high;
- author economic participation: traditional-like vs materially higher sensitivity;
- lower/base/higher sales realization;
- refund/cancellation event;
- high returns;
- production overrun;
- delayed distribution receipts.

## Controls
All contributions, deliverables, refund terms, sales obligations, rights, royalty/revenue share, representations, and cancellation mechanics require later governance and professional review. Benchmark package prices are not Ekewaka prices. Lane B remains segregated from Lane A acquisition and Lane C services to control RISK-002 and RISK-009.
