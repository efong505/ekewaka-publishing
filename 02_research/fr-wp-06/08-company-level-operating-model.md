# FR-WP-06 — Company-Level Operating Model

## Structure
### Fixed/recurring categories
Software/platform/admin, accounting/bookkeeping, professional services, insurance, compliance, baseline marketing/admin, data/audit systems, and any future facility/compensation costs only when supported.

### Variable categories
Title manufacturing, fulfillment, channel fees, returns, royalties, payment fees, contractors, service-delivery labor, customer support, freight, permissions, and title-specific marketing.

### One-time/contingent categories
Entity/legal setup, ISBN acquisition, systems implementation, migration, counsel, major catalog remediation, tooling, and exceptional rights/permissions work.

## Monthly/quarterly cash model
`Opening cash + cash receipts − operating cash outflow − title-development cash outflow − inventory/prepayments − contingent/professional outflow = ending cash`.

Track receivables timing by channel rather than treating sales as immediate cash.

## Break-even
`Company break-even recognized revenue = fixed operating cost / weighted contribution-margin %`, only after a defensible lane/channel mix exists.

Illustrative planning-only matrix:

## Capacity
Lane C capacity must constrain revenue: `billable/deliverable hours ≤ staffed capacity × realistic utilization`. Lane A title pipeline must separately constrain cash-at-risk and management bandwidth.
