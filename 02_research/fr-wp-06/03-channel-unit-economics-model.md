# FR-WP-06 — Channel Unit Economics Model

## Channel formulas
| Channel | Planning formula | Key unresolved items |
|---|---|---|
| KDP Amazon POD | `LP × applicable payout − print − royalty − other variable` | live title print cost, marketplace, format |
| KDP Expanded | `LP × 40% − print − royalty − other variable` | live eligibility/cost; slower cash timing |
| Ingram trade/POD | `LP × (1−wholesale discount) − print − current fees − expected returns − royalty` | live calculator, discount, return policy |
| Lulu Global | `80% × (LP×50% − print)` before royalty/other title costs | live title print cost/policy |
| Direct POD | `customer receipts − print − fulfillment − processor − shipping subsidy − refunds − tax/GRT − support − royalty` | provider/account/CPA validation |
| Stocked direct inventory | `receipts − landed unit cost − storage − pick/pack − payment − shrink − returns − support − royalty` | vendor/3PL quotes, sell-through |
| Events/churches/institutions/bulk | `net invoice receipts − landed units − freight/handling − discounts − event/channel cost` | actual discount/freight/payment terms |
| Short-run/offset | channel formula plus inventory/freight/storage/carrying cost | apples-to-apples quotes required |

## Illustrative arithmetic only
Using modeled LP `$18.99` and synthetic `$4.60` manufacturing for comparability:
- KDP 60%: `$6.79` pre-royalty.
- KDP Expanded: `$3.00` pre-royalty.
- Ingram-style 55% discount: `$3.95` before return exposure/fees.
- Lulu Global formula: `$3.92` before royalty/other costs.
- Synthetic direct (3%+$0.30 processor; $2.50 fulfillment): `$11.02` before royalty/refunds/tax/shipping subsidy/overhead.

The direct advantage is not free margin: operational costs and liabilities migrate from the distributor to Ekewaka.

## Volume analysis
Required quantity anchors `1/10/25/50/100/250/500/1,000/5,000+` remain in the model. Short-run/offset comparisons cannot be completed with decision-grade economics until live identical-spec quotes, freight, storage and fulfillment are captured.
