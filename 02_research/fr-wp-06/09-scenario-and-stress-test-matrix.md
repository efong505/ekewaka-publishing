# FR-WP-06 — Scenario and Stress-Test Matrix

All values below are modeled planning assumptions used to demonstrate mechanics; none are approved facts.

| Scenario | Fixed title investment | Weighted contribution/gross unit | Modeled break-even gross units | Dominant condition |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Downside | $12,000 | $1.75 | 6,858 | trade-heavy, higher returns/cost |
| Base/planning | $8,000 | $5.25 | 1,524 | mixed channel reference case |
| Upside | $5,000 | $8.00 | 625 | better direct mix/lower cost |
| Stress | $12,000 | $0.50 | 24,000 | severe returns/cost inflation/delay |

## Base reference mechanics
Modeled channel mix 50% KDP Amazon / 30% Ingram-style trade / 20% direct; modeled 10% royalty on defined publisher net receipts; modeled 15% trade-return sensitivity; synthetic $18.99 list and $4.60 comparability manufacturing assumption. Resulting weighted contribution is approximately $5.25/gross unit before company overhead.

## Required stress dimensions
Independently vary:
- return rate and return handling;
- manufacturing inflation;
- wholesale discount;
- direct-channel mix;
- processor/fulfillment cost;
- title fixed investment;
- royalty basis/rate;
- sales volume;
- delayed receivables;
- refund/chargeback rate;
- inventory sell-through;
- contractor/labor overrun.

## Interpretation
The model is nonlinear around thin trade margins: modest changes in returns, manufacturing, discount, or royalty can create a large change in break-even units. Stress cases are therefore mandatory before later underwriting decisions.
