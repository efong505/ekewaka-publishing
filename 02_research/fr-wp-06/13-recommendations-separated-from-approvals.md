# FR-WP-06 — Recommendations Separated from Approvals

## Recommendations for later consideration
1. Maintain a channel-specific title P&L rather than a single blended royalty/margin number.
2. Require downside and stress cases before any outside-author underwriting decision.
3. Treat returns reserve and cash timing as first-class title economics.
4. Capture live landed-cost evidence before any print, price, discount, inventory or procurement decision.
5. Keep Lane B research-only unless separately governed and professionally reviewed.
6. For Lane C, complete FR-WP-04 and bottom-up labor/vendor costing before public package design or pricing.
7. Build future ISBN/metadata/rights and royalty-accounting registries with reconciliation/audit fields from inception.
8. Use reserve formulas that combine operating burn, title pipeline, returns/inventory exposure, and contingent professional costs.
9. Re-run the model immediately before material decisions using current vendor/platform terms.

## Not approvals
None of the above approves a retail price, royalty, advance, acquisition, author-service price, reserve amount, vendor, printer, distributor, fulfillment provider, ISBN block, rights transaction, contract, tax/accounting treatment, Lane B/Lane C launch, production system, paid marketing, or cash commitment.
