# FR-WP-03 — Risk Findings

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-10

## Existing governed risks materially affected

### RISK-003 — Unsustainable trade-title economics — Critical
Trade contribution can be compressed by print cost, high wholesale discount, returns, author royalty and title investment. Ingram trade-friendly terms can make a title economically fragile if list price/page count are not aligned.

**Control:** FR-WP-06 must model channel contribution separately and include stress cases before any acquisition economics are accepted.

### RISK-007 — Distributor / returns exposure — High
Ingram returnable status can create later wholesale-cost chargebacks; Yes-Deliver adds published handling/shipping charges. A title may appear profitable before returns reverse cash.

**Control:** explicit return reserve, return timing, wholesale-value exposure, and policy sensitivity.

### RISK-010 — Publisher cash-flow strain — High
Offset/short-run inventory requires cash before sell-through. Trade payouts arrive later, and returns may arrive after original compensation.

**Control:** cash-conversion schedule, inventory cap, staged reprints, no speculative large runs without evidence-backed demand.

### RISK-015 — Vendor / platform dependency — Medium/High
KDP, Ingram and Lulu have different reach, economics and operational controls. Overdependence on one provider can expose Ekewaka to policy, pricing or account disruptions.

**Control:** preserve multi-channel architecture, publisher-controlled metadata/ISBN records, exportable operating records and substitute-vendor capability.

## Additional FR-WP-03 operating risks

### DIST-RISK-01 — False unit-cost comparison — High
A quoted manufacturing price can appear cheaper while excluding freight, setup, proofing, storage or fulfillment.

**Control:** compare landed cost on identical title specifications.

### DIST-RISK-02 — Overstock / obsolescence — High
Large print runs can trap cash in unsold copies, especially if metadata, cover, corrections or edition changes occur.

**Control:** demand threshold, staged print quantities, inventory aging reports and write-down policy later.

### DIST-RISK-03 — Freight volatility / damage — Medium/High
Offset and short-run savings can be erased by freight, pallet handling or transit damage.

**Control:** freight-inclusive quotes, packaging standards, damage/reprint terms and contingency allowance.

### DIST-RISK-04 — Returns-liability concentration — High
A trade-heavy title with high returnability can create a concentrated cash reversal.

**Control:** return reserve by title/channel; stress testing; no use of sales shipped as equivalent to final sell-through.

### DIST-RISK-05 — Metadata / duplicate-channel conflict — High
Using multiple print/distribution platforms without controlled ISBN/metadata rules can create conflicting listings or duplicate editions.

**Control:** FR-WP-05 central edition/ISBN/metadata authority and documented channel setup.

### DIST-RISK-06 — Direct-commerce compliance gap — High
Direct DTC improves gross revenue potential but moves responsibility toward Ekewaka for checkout, tax/GRT, refunds, privacy/customer data and support depending on architecture.

**Control:** implementation-specific WEB/SYS/finance/CPA review before launch.

### DIST-RISK-07 — Local vendor assumption without validation — Medium
Local proximity does not establish bookstore-quality manufacturing, competitive cost or fulfillment capability.

**Control:** sample/proof evaluation, identical-spec quotes, references and written service terms before selection.

### DIST-RISK-08 — Premium-format margin erosion — High
Color interiors, hardcovers, workbooks, specialty paper and finishing can increase manufacturing and freight sharply.

**Control:** format-specific pricing floors and direct/bulk channel analysis; avoid generic paperback assumptions.

## Risk disposition
No existing governed risk is closed or downgraded by FR-WP-03. These findings are inputs for PM/VER-01 and later FR-WP-06.
