## Evidence limitations
- Public printer examples are not comparable unless trim, page count, paper, ink, binding, finish, proofing, freight and quantity are identical.
- Marketing statements such as “lower cost” or “global reach” are treated as vendor claims unless the underlying economics/mechanics are independently calculable.
- Current Ingram print cost and fee schedules should be captured from live calculators/account pricing before FR-WP-06 finalization.
- No confidential vendor economics were inferred.
