# FR-WP-03 — Recommendations Separated From Approvals

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-10

## Recommendation 1 — Use a multi-channel baseline
**Recommend:** Treat KDP Print, IngramSpark, and a direct-commerce path as complementary rather than mutually exclusive architectures.
- KDP: Amazon-native POD.
- Ingram: bookstore/library/trade wholesale availability.
- Direct: Lulu Direct or stocked inventory/fulfillment depending volume.

**Not approved:** No vendor selection, account commitment, distribution enrollment, pricing, or publication action is authorized.

## Recommendation 2 — Keep POD as default for uncertain demand
**Recommend:** For new/uncertain titles and quantities under roughly 25–50 units, default modeling should favor POD unless quality/specification needs justify otherwise.

**Not approved:** This is not a permanent procurement rule and does not prohibit short-run quotes.

## Recommendation 3 — Quote short-run at known event/bulk demand
**Recommend:** At 25/50/100/250-unit known requirements, compare KDP/Ingram/Lulu landed author/publisher-copy cost with at least one local/regional and one national short-run quote.

**Not approved:** No bulk order may be placed without applicable Human Owner approval.

## Recommendation 4 — Introduce offset comparison at 250–500+
**Recommend:** At 250–500 units, require at least one offset quote. At 1,000+ units, offset should normally be included in the primary comparison set.

**Not approved:** Offset is not presumed superior; landed cost, lead time, quality, storage and sell-through must support it.

## Recommendation 5 — Treat Ingram returns as contingent liability
**Recommend:** Any future returnable trade policy should carry a title-level reserve modeled against wholesale value and timing, with high-return stress scenarios.

**Not approved:** DIST-01 does not select a final returnability option or reserve percentage.

## Recommendation 6 — Do not blindly adopt 55% wholesale discount
**Recommend:** FR-WP-06 should model 55% as the trade-oriented case plus lower-discount sensitivities. MKT/sales work should later determine where bookstore stocking is actually strategic.

**Not approved:** No final wholesale discount is established.

## Recommendation 7 — Model direct sales as its own business channel
**Recommend:** Direct contribution should include payment processing, ecommerce platform, print/fulfillment, shipping subsidy, refunds, chargebacks, tax/GRT, customer support and inventory/warehouse costs where applicable.

**Not approved:** No direct storefront or merchant-of-record architecture is selected or launched.

## Recommendation 8 — Use New Mexico vendors as formal quote candidates
**Recommend:** First local quote set should include Aiken Printing because its public capabilities most closely match short-run digital + offset + perfect binding + inventory/fulfillment. Also solicit comparable book-specific quotes from at least one of Starline, Graphic Sky, Paper Tiger, Ortiz, The Printer's Press or BPS depending format.

**Not approved:** Inclusion as a quote candidate is not vendor endorsement or selection.

## Recommendation 9 — Preserve publisher control over records
**Recommend:** Regardless of printer/distributor, Ekewaka should retain authoritative internal records for ISBN/edition identity, metadata, pricing history, channel configuration, print files, vendor quotes, inventory and returns.

**Not approved:** ISBN ownership/edition rules remain FR-WP-05 scope.

## Recommendation 10 — Use common quote specifications
**Recommend:** All quote comparisons use the identical title specification and explicitly normalize setup, proof, freight, receiving, storage and fulfillment.

## Recommendation 11 — Gate large inventory by evidence-backed demand
**Recommend:** 500/1,000/5,000+ orders require documented demand basis, sell-through scenario, storage/fulfillment plan, cash requirement and downside case before Human Owner review.

**Not approved:** No bulk-print threshold automatically authorizes an order.

## Recommendation 12 — Route package to VER-01
**Recommend:** PM-01 should launch an independent VER-01 review focused on source freshness, vendor-term accuracy, arithmetic, returns treatment, modeled-vs-verified separation, completeness of FR-WP-06 inputs, and preservation of Human Owner gates.

## Overall posture
These recommendations support a staged, evidence-driven distribution architecture. They do not convert foundational research into an operating decision.
