# FR-WP-04 — Recommendations Separated From Approvals

**Status:** SERV-01 research recommendations only. None are Human Owner approvals.

## Recommended for later Phase 4 architecture

1. Build Lane C from separately costed service modules first; packages should be bounded combinations of those modules rather than opaque copied competitor bundles.
2. Define editorial services by level, word count, manuscript condition, pass count and explicit exclusions. Do not sell an undefined "editing included" promise.
3. Use complexity bands for cover/interior/ebook production and charge separately for materially new creative direction or post-approval content changes.
4. Include project-management and QA labor explicitly in cost models and capacity planning.
5. Prefer customer-controlled ISBNs, publisher identity, KDP/Ingram/retailer accounts and direct revenue routing where practicable.
6. Treat distribution setup as administrative/platform work, not bookstore/library placement.
7. Treat websites, publicity and marketing support as defined deliverables/time allowances with no sales, review, ranking, bestseller or placement guarantees.
8. Distinguish correction of provider errors from customer-requested revisions/change orders.
9. Define source-file, stock-art, font, illustration and third-party-license handoff explicitly.
10. Maintain live price/source dates and recheck competitor/vendor/platform economics immediately before any public-price decision.
11. Replace FR-WP-06 modeled utilization, PM/QA, refund/chargeback and vendor assumptions with measured pilot/dry-run data before commercial scale.
12. Require counsel and CPA/tax review before final service agreement, refunds/cancellations, account-access architecture or public launch.
