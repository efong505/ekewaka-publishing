# FR-WP-04 — Marketing, Website & Consulting Services Benchmark

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-15

## 1. Author websites

Reedsy reports that 2025 website-design collaborations on its marketplace averaged about **$1,640**. Fewer than 10% were below $500 and more than 10% exceeded $2,500. Scope can include branding, newsletter integration, SEO, ecommerce links, event pages and other functions.

EFA's 2026 member survey reports website work around **$50/hour**, with a median reported project value around **$500**. These figures describe a different survey population and should not be averaged mechanically with Reedsy.

Destiny House includes a website in higher package tiers, but its current public package-price inconsistency makes those package stickers unsuitable as a reliable website-value benchmark.

## 2. Publicity and launch support

Reedsy reports a median of about **$1,370** for a month-long book-publicity campaign on its marketplace. Publicity scope can include positioning, pitch/bio/press materials, media research and outreach. It is professional effort, not a promise of resulting coverage.

BookBaby publicly offers marketing products/services within its broader self-publishing ecosystem. Such provider claims should be evaluated by exact deliverables rather than by phrases such as "marketing included."

Destiny House package scopes show common launch-asset bundling patterns: e-flyers, postcards, websites, trailers, retractable banners and posters at higher tiers.

## 3. Consulting, coaching and project management

EFA's 2026 survey reports:
- coaching: roughly **$75–$100/hour**, median project around $1,000;
- project management: roughly **$65–$75/hour**, median project around $1,250.

Destiny House publishes a writing-coach offer starting at **$75/week** for a constrained weekly review of approximately 500 words plus notes/accountability communications.

Gatekeeper assigns an Author Manager through the production cycle and for 30 days after publication, illustrating that author coordination itself can be a substantial bundled service.

## 4. Scope controls — websites

Any future website service should define:
- page count;
- template versus custom design;
- author/book count;
- forms/newsletter/ecommerce integrations;
- domain purchase/ownership;
- hosting ownership and recurring fees;
- copywriting and image sourcing;
- SEO scope;
- analytics setup;
- accessibility baseline;
- revision rounds;
- maintenance/support period;
- handoff credentials and source assets.

## 5. Scope controls — marketing/publicity

Any future marketing support should define concrete work products or time allowances, for example:
- launch strategy session;
- launch checklist/timeline;
- defined number of social/graphic assets;
- press kit or sell sheet;
- media-list research;
- defined outreach campaign duration and contact count where appropriate;
- ad-account setup versus ad spend;
- bookstore/library outreach preparation versus actual placement;
- metadata optimization;
- reporting/closeout.

Excluded unless separately contracted: paid media budget, guaranteed media pickup, reviews, sales volume, rankings, bestseller status, bookstore stocking, library acquisition or event attendance.

## 6. Coaching boundary

Coaching should be time- or deliverable-bounded. It should not silently become developmental editing, ghostwriting, unlimited manuscript review, business/legal/tax advice or continuous on-call support.

## 7. FR-WP-06 variables

Model:
- web-design/development hours;
- software/theme/plugin/vendor costs;
- hosting/domain pass-through treatment;
- marketing asset count and design hours;
- publicity campaign hours/duration;
- consultation/coaching hours;
- project-management hours;
- revision rounds;
- post-launch support window;
- third-party ad/printing/postage spend separately from service labor.

No public marketing package, promise or price is approved by this benchmark.
