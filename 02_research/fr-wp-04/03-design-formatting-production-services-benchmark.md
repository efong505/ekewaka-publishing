# FR-WP-04 — Design, Formatting & Production Services Benchmark

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-15

## 1. Cover design

### Reedsy — current 2026 marketplace benchmark

Reedsy reports professional book-cover design averaging about **$880**, with most projects approximately **$625–$1,250**. Its genre examples place nonfiction around a **$700 median**. Complexity, illustration, series branding, multiple formats and extra revision cycles can increase cost.

Classification: **market benchmark**.

### Editorial Freelancers Association — 2026 rate survey

EFA reports book-cover graphic-design work around **$50/hour**, with a median reported project price around **$350**. This is a member-survey benchmark and not quality-normalized against full custom cover packages.

Classification: **professional-association market benchmark**.

## 2. Interior print layout / typesetting

EFA's 2026 survey reports book/print layout around **$50–$55/hour**, with reported throughput of roughly **5–11 pages/hour**. Complexity can rise materially with tables, footnotes, illustrations, workbook elements, indexes, unusual trim sizes, bleed, color and accessibility requirements.

Ebook Launch provides quote-based print formatting and states that its delivered print PDF is prepared for platforms including KDP Print and IngramSpark. Its formatting service does not itself supply an ISBN; the customer can supply one for placement in the book. Index creation and cover work are separate services.

Classification: EFA = **market benchmark**; Ebook Launch = **verified current provider scope**.

## 3. Ebook formatting

A defensible Lane C ebook scope should define:
- input manuscript/file requirements;
- reflowable versus fixed-layout treatment;
- images, tables, footnotes/endnotes and hyperlinks;
- linked table of contents;
- device/platform testing assumptions;
- accessibility treatment;
- number of distribution formats/files supplied;
- whether later text changes are included;
- whether upload/account setup is included separately.

Print and ebook formatting should not be treated as one labor unit merely because both are called "formatting."

## 4. Revision and change-order benchmark

Ebook Launch publishes unusually clear change mechanics:
- active-project changes to the approved cover direction can be incorporated during the design process;
- a new creative direction after approval can become a new project/quote;
- post-final formatting changes are generally charged by revision/format, with published minimum charges;
- the first correction submission is free when the correction relates to formatting/provider error;
- a large revision batch (30+ revisions) can trigger full reformat pricing.

Gatekeeper also publishes post-production controls, including:
- certain additional edits/reformatting at **$125/hour**;
- a cover post-template change fee;
- tiered content-change charges after production/publication.

These examples support separating **provider-error correction** from **author-requested scope change**.

## 5. Production-scope variables for FR-WP-06

Model independently:
- number of initial cover concepts;
- stock-art/license allowance;
- custom illustration allowance;
- typography/logo/series-branding needs;
- paperback, hardcover and ebook format count;
- interior page count;
- simple-text versus complex-layout band;
- tables/images/footnotes/index/workbook complexity;
- proof rounds;
- revision rounds;
- author changes after approval;
- rush schedule;
- source-file handoff;
- accessibility/alt-text requirements;
- print proof/copy and shipping costs.

## 6. Recommended control posture — not approval

Do not default to unlimited creative revisions. A later Ekewaka service design should distinguish:
1. correction of Ekewaka/vendor production errors;
2. included author revision rounds;
3. material author changes after approval;
4. new creative direction or format expansion.

The number of included rounds and any fees remain Human Owner decision variables after cost validation and counsel review.
