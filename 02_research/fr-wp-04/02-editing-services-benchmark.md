# FR-WP-04 — Editing Services Benchmark

**Retrieval date:** 2026-09-15

## Service taxonomy

Lane C must distinguish at minimum:
- manuscript/editorial assessment;
- developmental editing;
- line editing;
- copyediting;
- proofreading;
- fact-checking/citation review support where separately scoped.

"Editing" alone is not a sufficient package term.

## Current market evidence

### Reedsy — 2026 marketplace benchmark

Reedsy reports professional book editors generally around **$0.01–$0.05 per word**, with a more specific 2026 table:

| Service | Reedsy 2026 reported range |
|---|---:|
| Editorial assessment | $0.018–$0.029/word |
| Developmental editing | $0.026–$0.053/word |
| Copy editing | $0.020–$0.032/word |
| Proofreading | $0.014–$0.021/word |

For a generic 80,000-word manuscript, Reedsy reports roughly $1,920–$4,560 depending on service/scope.

Classification: **marketplace benchmark**, not a fixed provider tariff.

### Editorial Freelancers Association — 2026 rate survey

For nonfiction, the EFA chart reports:

| Service | Per word | Per hour | Throughput |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Copyediting | 3.0¢–4.0¢ | $45–$60 | 5–9 pages/hour |
| Developmental editing | 4.0¢–5.0¢ | $57.50–$70 | 4–8 pages/hour |
| Formatting | about 2.0¢ | $60–$70 | 7–15 pages/hour |

The chart uses 250 words/page. EFA states that it does not set rates and that individual rates vary with expertise, turnaround, complexity and other factors.

Classification: **professional-association market benchmark**.

### Gatekeeper Press — explicit service pricing

Current public Gatekeeper pricing includes:
- proofreading, two rounds: **$25 per 1,000 words**;
- copy edit + proofreading: **$35 per 1,000 words**;
- broader developmental + copy + proof options are available through its quote/package flow;
- additional edits in several contexts: **$125/hour**.

Classification: **verified current provider price/scope**.

### Destiny House Publishing — package editing

Silver/Platinum product pages currently advertise "copy editing and proofing" for **50–150 pages**; Diamond advertises **50–199 pages**. The pages do not provide enough detail to determine editorial depth, number of passes, manuscript-condition assumptions or exact labor method.

Classification: **verified current package scope, insufficient for professional-rate equivalence**.

## Labor implications for FR-WP-06

Using EFA throughput only as a planning benchmark:
- 80,000-word nonfiction copyedit at 5–9 pages/hour implies roughly **36–64 labor hours**.
- 80,000-word nonfiction developmental edit at 4–8 pages/hour implies roughly **40–80 labor hours**.

These are derived benchmark calculations, not Ekewaka staffing commitments.

## Scope controls required before any Ekewaka offer

Every editorial service should define:
- service type;
- manuscript word ceiling;
- manuscript-condition assumption or sample review;
- style-guide baseline;
- included pass count;
- whether author questions/consultation are included;
- whether post-author-revision re-read is included;
- fact-checking/citation/permissions exclusions;
- specialist-content exclusions;
- turnaround assumption;
- rush treatment;
- change-order trigger;
- delivery format and Track Changes/comment handling.

## Key risk

Bundling an undefined editorial promise into a package is the highest-probability path to negative Lane C contribution margin.
