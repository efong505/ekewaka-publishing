## 2. Derived labor-hour sensitivities

Using the EFA convention of 250 words/page and its reported throughput only as planning benchmarks:

- 80,000-word nonfiction = approximately 320 pages.
- Copyedit at 5–9 pages/hour = approximately **36–64 hours**.
- Developmental editing at 4–8 pages/hour = approximately **40–80 hours**.
- Formatting at 7–15 pages/hour = approximately **21–46 hours**.

These are derived benchmark ranges, not Ekewaka promises or staffing standards. Manuscript condition and complexity can materially change them.

## 4. Project-management / QA sensitivity

No universal market percentage was found for PM/QA overhead. FR-WP-06 should keep actual PM/QA as separately estimated hours. For stress testing only, it may test **10% / 20% / 30% of direct service labor hours** as modeled planning sensitivities until dry-run/pilot data exist.

Classification: **modeled assumption only**.

## 5. Revision / change-order sensitivity

Model at least:
- included revision rounds: configurable Human Owner/service-design variable;
- 0 / 1 / 2 additional unbilled rounds as downside sensitivities;
- provider-error correction excluded from customer change-order revenue;
- post-approval content changes separately estimated;
- full rework trigger for major creative-direction or manuscript changes.

## 6. Payment, refund and chargeback variables

Observed market patterns include a 50% deposit option, full payment, monthly installments and payment before production. These observations do not establish Ekewaka policy.

FR-WP-06 variables:
- deposit percentage — **Human Owner decision variable**;
- installment count/timing — **Human Owner decision variable**;
- milestone-based earned/nonrefundable amount — unresolved pending counsel/accounting review;
- processor fee — live validation required;
- refund loss — actual policy + observed data required;
- chargeback loss — actual processor/operating data required.

For stress testing only, model combined refund/chargeback loss at **0% / 2% / 5% of collected service revenue** until evidence replaces it.

Classification: **modeled assumption only**.

## 7. Capacity / utilization sensitivity

A service business cannot safely assume 100% billable labor because PM, sales/admin, rework, scheduling, QA, leave and idle time consume capacity. Until Ekewaka measures actual operations, FR-WP-06 may stress-test **50% / 65% / 80% billable utilization** for internal labor planning.

Classification: **modeled assumption only**; not a staffing target.

## 8. Package versus à-la-carte variables

For each module and package track:
- standalone fully loaded cost;
- incremental package coordination cost/saving;
- vendor allowance;
- included copies/shipping;
- expected revision burden;
- expected support hours;
- payment timing;
- cancellation exposure;
- utilization effect;
- cross-subsidy between service modules.

## 9. Margin-floor control

FR-WP-06 already contains a mechanical price-floor formula. FR-WP-04 supplies better cost/scope inputs but **does not set a gross-margin floor**. The margin floor remains a Human Owner decision after vendor quotes, labor validation, tax/accounting review and market validation.
