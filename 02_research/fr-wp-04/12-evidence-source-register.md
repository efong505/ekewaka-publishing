## Evidence handling notes

- Public prices are volatile and require live recheck before material decisions.
- Destiny House currently has an unresolved cross-page price inconsistency; the scope is usable as a comparator, but its sticker prices are not a durable anchor.
- Reedsy and EFA data are market benchmarks, not confidential competitor economics and not approved Ekewaka vendor rates.
- No confidential provider economics were inferred.
- No source is used to imply sales, bestseller status, review volume, bookstore placement, library acquisition or other guaranteed commercial outcome.
