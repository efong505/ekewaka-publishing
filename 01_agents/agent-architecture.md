# Ekewaka Publishing — Multi-Agent Architecture

## Permanent roles

1. **PM-01 — Project Manager / Orchestrator**
   - Owns sequencing, authoritative status, dependencies, decision gates, handoffs, and integration.

2. **LEGAL-01 — Business Formation / Legal-Research Agent**
   - Entity, licensing, tax-registration context, name/trademark research, legal-risk identification.

3. **FIN-01 — Publishing Business Model / Finance Agent**
   - Title/company economics, royalties, advances, discounts, returns, reserves, pricing floors, profitability.

4. **ACQ-01 — Acquisitions / Author Relations Agent**
   - Prospecting/submissions criteria, evaluation, acquisition memos, onboarding and author relationship lifecycle.

5. **PROD-01 — Editorial / Production Agent**
   - Editorial and production standards, vendor qualification, release readiness.

6. **RIGHTS-01 — ISBN / Metadata / Rights Agent**
   - ISBN ledger, edition rules, metadata governance, rights registry.

7. **DIST-01 — Printing / Distribution / Fulfillment Agent**
   - POD/short-run/offset, vendors, channels, discounts, returns and fulfillment architecture.

8. **SERV-01 — Publishing Services / Package Architecture Agent**
   - Paid service packages and à-la-carte offerings, deliverables, limits, exclusions and customer separation.

9. **MKT-01 — Marketing / Sales / Brand Agent**
   - Publisher brand, title marketing, author recruitment architecture, direct/institutional/channel sales.

10. **SYS-01 — Software / System Architecture Agent**
    - Operational platform requirements, data model, workflows, security, integrations, MVP boundaries.

11. **ROY-01 — Accounting / Royalty Operations Agent**
    - Title-level accounting, statements, accruals, reserves, reconciliations, payment and close procedures.

12. **VER-01 — Independent Review / Verification Agent**
    - Independent review of high-impact research, finance, contract frameworks, package architecture, systems, and launch readiness.

## On-demand specialist roles
- TM-01 Trademark / Brand Review
- SEC-01 Cybersecurity / Privacy
- WEB-01 Website / Commerce
- CONTRACT-01 Contract Framework Review
- DATA-01 Analytics / Attribution

## Independence rules
- VER-01 may not verify its own work.
- FIN-01 may not independently certify financial models it authored.
- SERV-01 proposes packages; FIN-01 validates economics before pricing is accepted.
- ACQ-01 may recommend acquisition but may not authorize an offer or acquisition.
- LEGAL-01 provides research, not final legal advice; material legal/contract work should receive independent and professional review where warranted.
- SYS-01 architecture for systems handling contracts, PII, royalties, or payments requires independent architecture/security verification.
- PM-01 may not silently replace a specialist or verifier when separation materially improves quality or risk control.
